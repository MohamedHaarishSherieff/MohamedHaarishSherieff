### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **AI/ML Engineer and Data Science Postgraduate** currently pursuing my **MSc in Data Science at Reva University, Bengaluru**, with an academic foundation in **BSc Computer Science (AI & ML Specialization)** from **Vels University, Chennai**.

I bridge the gap between bleeding-edge machine learning research (computer vision, deep learning, NLP, and multimodal LLMs) and high-performance, production-ready mobile and web applications. My work spans building autonomous legal intelligence courtrooms for the **Meta Open Source Hackathon**, engineering cross-state healthcare mobile platforms, and training robust deep learning vision pipelines.

- 🎓 **Education:** Master of Science in Data Science (2025–2027) &bull; Bachelor of Science in CS - AI & ML (2022–2025)
- 🔭 **Currently Building:** Autonomous multimodal agentic workflows, production LLM pipelines, and computer vision systems
- 🧠 **Research Interests:** Open-weights foundation models, generative AI in statutory governance, multimodal deep learning & medical vision
- 💬 **Ask Me About:** Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, React Native, Next.js, LLM fine-tuning & prompt orchestration, Firebase, GCP
